Privacy Policy for Tokutei Hotel Exam

Last updated: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your personal information when you use the Tokutei Hotel Exam application on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect basic information such as your name, email address, and profile picture when you use Google Sign-In or Sign-In with Apple. This data is exclusively used to track your exam progress, save quiz history, and generate certificates within the app.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with trusted third-party services to ensure security and performance. These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services (for Android devices)
  • Apple App Store Services (for iOS devices)
  • Firebase Authentication (to manage secure user accounts)
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (to monitor app performance and fix bugs)

3. Data Security & Retention

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information. We secure it using industry-standard methods. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Account and Data Deletion (Apple Requirement)

We respect your right to privacy. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data (including quiz progress and history), you can easily do so at any time directly through the Account / Profile Settings inside the application. Alternatively, you can request data deletion by contacting us via email.
